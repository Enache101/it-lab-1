A basic local check against your own assignment brief. Your text stays in this browser. This does not grade your work, check plagiarism or validate references.

For Word or PDF, copy the main text and paste below. Scanned documents need text recognition first.

Finding a phrase does not prove the requirement is met. Check theory application, evidence, reference accuracy and formatting yourself or with your teacher.

Add IT lab 1 to your device

Keep an icon on your phone Home Screen or in your computer’s apps. Choose your device below. You decide whether to add it.

iPhone or iPad
  1. Open IT lab 1 in Safari. If you opened it inside another app, copy its link and open Safari first.
  2. Open Safari’s Share menu (the square with an upward arrow). On some layouts, open the More menu first.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. Scroll through the actions if needed.
  4. If offered, leave Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add.
Android phone or tablet
  1. Open the website in Chrome or another browser that supports web apps.
  2. If the Install button above appears, tap it and confirm in the browser.
  3. Otherwise open your browser’s menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app, if available. Confirm Add or Install.
Windows laptop, Chromebook or desktop
  1. Open the site in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Use the Install button above when available, or the browser’s install icon/menu.
  3. If your browser offers only a shortcut, use its Create shortcut option. You can also bookmark the website.

The available options depend on your browser and device. Adding an app does not always create a desktop icon automatically.

Mac laptop or desktop

In Safari on macOS Sonoma 14 or later, choose File → Add to Dock (or Share → Add to Dock), then Add. In Chrome or Edge, use the Install button above or the browser’s install menu when available. On older Safari versions, bookmark the site.

First open the app online and wait for “Offline learning download is ready”. Quizzes and flashcards can then work offline. University links and online services still need internet.

Saved work can differ between a browser and its installed app. Download a study backup in Settings before switching, then restore it in the app if needed.
